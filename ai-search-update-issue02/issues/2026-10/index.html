Issue 02 · November 2026

01 · AI search at a glance

A record month for Human Referrals: organic vs paid

Year to date, to 31 Oct 2026. October was the first month with ChatGPT Ads live, so this issue reports organic and paid Human Referrals separately.

What it means

Of the 15.5K people AI sent to HERE.com in October, about 10.7K clicked a ChatGPT ad and about 4.8K arrived organically, a third more than in September. Visibility now reads 29.4%, and HERE ranks #4 among competitors, down from the #2 we reported for September. That move comes from the October prompt review, which retired 161 of our 500 prompts: on the new set, September reads #4 too.

02 · AI search scorecard · October 2026

Five AI Search terms we use throughout this update

We currently track 339 buyer-style questions across 19 topics, asked daily of nine AI engines.

How to read the numbers

Prompt

One question we track, worded the way a buyer would ask it in an AI search engine.

Topic

A group of related prompts, such as "Fleet Routing" or "Location AI agents". We track 19 topics.

Human Referrals

Real people who visited HERE.com after clicking a link in an AI chat response. Organic unless marked paid.

Visibility Score

Share of AI answers that name HERE. 40% means HERE appears in 40 of every 100 answers on those prompts.

Rank

Where HERE's visibility sits against Google Maps Platform, Mapbox, TomTom and other brands.

1

The search shift: organic down, AI climbing, paid in the mix

Traditional organic traffic fell year on year while Human Referrals from AI search grew into a measurable channel. The last column compares October with September.

Insight

Organic traffic keeps sliding: visitors fell from 207K in Q1 to 180K in Q3, and another 10% in October.

AI search grew alongside it: Profound's organic AI traffic rose from 2.9K in Q1 to 12.9K in Q3, and ChatGPT Ads added 10.7K paid visits in October.

Insight

AI organic is the smallest channel but brings the best-quality visits: the lowest bounce rate (28.3%), the most pages per visit (2.04), the highest share of high-value users (29.2%) and a 3.7% form-submit rate, more than three times SEO and paid search.

4

Which pages AI sends people to

Being cited and being clicked are two different things. The organic picture is unchanged: /products/wego and the homepage lead. New this month, the ChatGPT Ads landing pages enter the top ten on visits, with their paid clicks shown in their own column.

Insight

Three of the top six pages by visits are October's ad landing pages (/platform/routing, /map-data, /map-rendering): 7.2K of their 9K visits are paid clicks, and they have fewer than 700 AI citations between them. On the organic side, /products/wego and the homepage still lead, /developer keeps converting above its weight, and /contact and /why-here are each cited 9K times yet earn fewer than 300 visits.

1 Jan–31 Oct 2026. AI Citations = AI assistant bot visits to the page. Paid Referrals = ChatGPT Ads clicks by landing page (Profound Ads Manager, October); organic Human Referrals = Profound's human visits to the page minus those clicks. Source: Profound Agent Analytics and Ads Manager.

03 · Updates

Recent updates and next steps

1. Developer Query Focus → part of the DX strategy

Profound surfaced that "developer how-to" questions with technical depth make up 42.3% of a survey of 12,830 relevant market prompts. That finding now sits inside the Developer Experience (DX) strategy the Web Experience team is working on: Wave 1 covers ten developer-facing pages, each mapped to its own set of Profound prompts and to developer-specific FAQs.

2. AI-powered article creation

The HERE360 team has built an AEO topic agent that suggests up to 10 blog topics a month, based on HERE's share of voice and visibility against competitors, and a second agent that drafts articles on those topics. The team is targeting 11 AEO-optimised blog posts in November with this workflow, to raise content output and strengthen HERE's visibility in AI search.

3. AEO Action Plan rollout

We've started building action plans in Profound to share with the teams we work with, including HERE360, Developer Advocacy, Documentation and PPMK, so we can agree monthly targets and content improvements faster. The work has to reach beyond HERE.com, because AI engines draw their citations from across the web.
