01 · AI search at a glance

AI is now a meaningful acquisition channel for HERE

Year to date, to 30 Sep 2026. Citations and human referrals have scaled sharply since Q1. The same four numbers we track in the AI Search Performance deck.

What it means

Citations and clicks are scaling fast. Visibility has dipped because more competitors now appear in the same answers, not because we are cited less.

02 · AI search scorecard · Q3 2026

Where we stand in AI answers

The Web Experience team's AI search scorecard, updated with Profound data to 30 Sep 2026. We track 500 buyer-style questions across 19 topics, asked daily of nine AI engines.

How to read the numbers

Prompt

One question we track, worded the way a buyer would ask it in an AI search engine.

Topic

A group of related prompts. "Fleet Routing" holds 45 of them. We track 19 topics.

Visibility score

Share of AI answers that name HERE. 40% means HERE appears in 40 of every 100 answers on those prompts.

Rank

Where HERE's visibility sits against Google Maps Platform, Mapbox, TomTom and other brands.

AI citations

How many times AI mentions or links back to HERE in its answers.

Human referrals

Real people who visited HERE.com after clicking a link in an AI chat response.

Share of voice

The brand's share of all brand mentions across tracked answers.

Average position

The order in which the brand is named in an answer. Lower is better.

1

The search shift: organic down, AI climbing

Traditional organic traffic fell year on year while AI referrals went from almost nothing to a measurable channel.

So what

AI has won back 28K of the 77K visitors organic lost, and Q3 was the biggest quarter yet for AI referrals. Buyers now arrive having done their research in ChatGPT, so our pages need to answer on pricing, comparisons and proof.

Sources: Adobe Analytics (organic); Profound (AI referrals, to 30 Sep 2026, Profound's rounded totals). *Conversion figures as reported in the 24 Sep update.

2

Quarter by quarter (cumulative, year to date)

Every signal except visibility has compounded through the year. Hover a point for the value.

So what

Citations are converting: 232.9K citations and 28.2K real visits back to the site. Share is cooling, not presence: visibility eased from ~46% to ~35% while citations grew more than five-fold; more competitors entered the same answer pool. Bot activity is the fastest-growing signal: AI crawlers are indexing our content more efficiently.

*Q1 is partial: citation tracking began 17 Feb 2026. AI Citations = citation share × tracked citations, summed across the nine engines. Visibility Score is for the period, not cumulative (Sep = 1–30 Sep). Human Referrals and AI Bot Traffic are Profound's rounded totals. Source: Profound, to 30 Sep 2026.

5

Which pages AI sends people to

Being cited and being clicked are two different things. The pages that earn visits are product and platform pages, and /products/wego has now overtaken the homepage.

So what

Being cited isn't the same as being clicked: /contact and /why-here are among our most-cited pages and barely get a visit. Product and platform pages earn the visits, and ChatGPT sends around four in five of them (23.6K of 28.2K).

1 Jan–30 Sep 2026. AI citations here = AI assistant bot visits to the page; visits = human visits referred by AI. Source: Profound Agent Analytics.
